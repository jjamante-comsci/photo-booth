# Canva frame guide

Import `canva-layout-guide-1800x1200.svg` into Canva. Create a design with the **same custom size: 1800 × 1200 px**, place this guide at full size, and lock it.

Build your graphics in the white margins only. The colored rectangles are exactly where the current app draws the three photos:

- Photo 1: `x=90`, `y=96`, `1008 × 1008 px`
- Photo 2: `x=1152`, `y=96`, `540 × 468 px`
- Photo 3: `x=1152`, `y=636`, `540 × 468 px`

Before exporting the background, hide or delete the guide. Export as a PNG at 1800 × 1200 and import it into the app.

Note: the current app draws photos **on top of** the imported background. Do not put a decorative border over a photo area yet; the app needs a future foreground-overlay feature for that.
